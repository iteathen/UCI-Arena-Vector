# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and technically rigorous community. Participate with empathy, assume good faith while examining evidence, and critique ideas without attacking people.

Positive behavior includes:

- giving and accepting specific, actionable feedback;
- respecting different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles;
- making ownership, assumptions, uncertainty, and evidence explicit;
- crediting prior work and helping route contributions to the correct owner;
- protecting private information and reporting security concerns privately.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, deliberate misrepresentation, doxxing, credential disclosure, or retaliation against a good-faith reporter.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project spaces, and public interactions where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community.

Report GitHub-hosted abuse through [GitHub's private content-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). For non-confidential project moderation, flag the content and briefly request maintainer review without repeating harmful or private material. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of affected people. Retaliation and knowingly false reports are themselves violations. Good-faith disagreement or an incorrect technical proposal is not misconduct.
