# Governance

UCI Arena Vector is maintained in public under a maintainer-led model.

## Decision authority

The project owner is the final decision maker for releases, security response, repository administration, and accepted architecture. Decisions follow the authority and design hierarchy in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), not contributor seniority or implementation volume.

Material behavior and architecture decisions should be recorded in issues, accepted specifications, or architecture decision records. The authoritative owner of a fact or lifecycle must be explicit. Consensus is preferred; when consensus is not available, the maintainer records the decision and rationale so it can be revisited with new evidence.

## Contributor path

Anyone may report defects, join discussions, review public contracts, propose changes, and submit pull requests. Sustained contributors may be granted triage or write access when it reduces coordination cost without weakening review boundaries.

Repository access does not override review requirements. Contributors must disclose conflicts of interest relevant to a decision, follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), and use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities.

## Releases

Maintainers publish releases only from reviewed, validated commits on the protected default branch. Claims about correctness, compatibility, GPU support, performance, or playing strength require evidence for the exact published revisions and environment.
